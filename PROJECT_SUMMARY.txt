📊 PROJECT OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This is a complete, production-ready autonomous agent that:
• Scrapes content from multiple online sources
• Filters content by relevance to founding engineers
• Deduplicates and ranks articles by quality
• Clusters content by themes
• Generates beautiful newsletters in multiple formats
• Can run automatically on a schedule
